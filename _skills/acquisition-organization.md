### Summary
Acquisition and organization skills enabled the successful design and execution of complex neuroimaging studies, particularly the Human Connectome Phantom project. I coordinated longitudinal data collection across multiple MRI scanners, optimizing protocols for maximal data quality while ensuring participant safety and retention. Strong organizational capabilities facilitated the management of extensive multimodal acquisitions synchronized with physiological recordings.

### Representative work
- **HCPh acquisition**: Designed and executed comprehensive multimodal MRI acquisition protocol for the Human Connectome Phantom dataset, involving repeated scans of structural, diffusion, and functional MRI with simultaneous physiological monitoring [Thesis].
- **Scanner protocol design**: Developed optimized scanning protocols balancing data quality requirements with participant comfort, enabling longitudinal data collection without fatigue-induced artifacts [SOPs repository].
- **Longitudinal study coordination**: Organized and managed data collection for 12-24 session neuroimaging studies, ensuring consistent experimental conditions and high retention rates [Thesis, SOPs].
