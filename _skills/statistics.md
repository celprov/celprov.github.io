### Summary
Statistical analysis formed the foundation for rigorous evaluation of neuroimaging data across my thesis projects. I designed studies using repeated-measures ANOVA and MANOVA to account for individual variability, particularly in the Defacing and HCPh projects where temporal effects could confound results. fMRI activation analysis relied on general linear models to identify significant brain activations, while reliability estimation using intraclass correlation coefficients ensured consistent measurements across repeated acquisitions.

### Representative work
- **Repeated-measures ANOVA**: Applied to assess effects of defacing on human quality assessments, controlling for rater variability in T1-weighted MRI ratings [Paper submitted, PLOS Biology].
- **Activation analysis**: Developed GLM-based approaches for fMRI data analysis in breath-holding task paradigms, establishing cerebrovascular reactivity maps [Thesis].
- **Reliability estimation**: Used ICC metrics to quantify consistency of brain network measurements across scanner repetitions, enabling confidence intervals in connectivity analyses [Nature Methods Stage 1, 2023].
