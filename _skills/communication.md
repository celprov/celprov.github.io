### Summary
Effective communication was essential to disseminate research findings and establish collaborations throughout my thesis. I presented work at international conferences, delivered educational talks on quality control methodologies, and participated in training sessions. The public defense of my thesis represented the culmination of clear communication of complex neuroimaging concepts to diverse audiences, from technical experts to general academics.

### Representative work
- **FRRIM talk**: Presented thesis research on characterizing and improving MRI reliability at the Friday Radiology Research Interdisciplinary Meeting, Lausanne [Oral presentation, FRRIM 2023].
- **Public defense**: Defended PhD thesis on brain connectivity reliability assessment, demonstrating comprehensive understanding of neuroimaging methodologies and their clinical implications [Public defense, 2025].
- **Educational talk on QC**: Delivered oral presentation on functional MRI quality control at OHBM 2023, educating the neuroimaging community on best practices for reliable data analysis [Oral presentation, OHBM 2023].
- **Training session on QC**: Organized and led MRTool workshops on neuroimaging QA/QC, training researchers on practical implementation of quality assessment protocols [Workshop facilitator, MRTool].
- **Conference posters**: Presented multiple posters on topics including defacing biases in QA/QC and wavelet transforms for B1 field dynamics characterization [Posters: OHBM 2022, OHBM 2023, OHBM 2024, ISMRM 2022, ISMRM 2024].
