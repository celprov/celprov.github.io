<b> I just spent 3 fantastic days in Basel for PharmaCamp 2025!</b> &#129504;
<br>
On the first day, we gained valuable insights into careers in pharma from industry experts. The next two days were all about the hackathon, where CSEM challenged us to prototype either a smart pipette tip or a microneedle array. For me, this meant stepping into a whole new field of study, but I was excited to see how quickly I could grasp the scientific concepts with the help of my solid background.

I was truly impressed by the dedication of the 25 participants. In just 10 hours, we not only developed sound solutions to real-world problems, but also took on the roles of project managers and market analysts. Very inspiring! I feel especially grateful to have collaborated with an amazing and friendly team of four talented researchers. 

A huge thank you to the organisers for volunteering their time and making this awesome event possible!

This experience reinforced my conviction that I am passionate about innovation and that I thrive when working in a connected team towards a common goal.
