<b>Just back from three inspiring days in Boston, where I had the chance to collaboratively contribute to the NiPreps software ecosystem!</b>
<br>
I spent the time coding alongside like-minded peers, learning from domain experts, and expanding the scope of our tools.
My contribution was a deep-dive discussion on the needs to assess quality of Positron Emission Tomography (PET) imaging.
This led me to develop a new workflow—drawing inspiration from MRIQC’s functional processing—to generate quality reports tailored to PET.

Curious about the progress? You can follow the pull request [here](https://github.com/nipreps/mriqc/pull/1399)!

Also had the pleasure of reconnecting with old friends. Fun times ! :)
