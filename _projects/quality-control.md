# Technical Context
Quality control is essential for reliable neuroimaging analysis, yet standardized approaches were lacking in the field. Building on the available MRIQC and fMRIPrep tools, we developed comprehensive quality control protocols that integrate multiple assessment checkpoints throughout the data processing workflow. Our approach adopted the Swiss cheese model of security systems, implementing layered QC procedures to minimize the risk of including substandard data that could bias research outcomes.

## What I did
- Developed standardized quality control protocols for unprocessed and preprocessed functional and structural MRI data
- Implemented visual assessment procedures using MRIQC-generated reports for systematic quality evaluation
- Created exclusion criteria tailored to application-specific requirements in neuroimaging studies
- Enhanced fMRIPrep with additional quality assessment tools, including improved visualization of motion artifacts
- Organized and presented educational content on quality control best practices at international conferences

## Methods / Stack
- **Quality Assessment Tools**: MRIQC for unprocessed data inspection, fMRIPrep for preprocessed data evaluation
- **Visualization Improvements**: Enhanced carpet plot with "crown" region for detecting motion-related artifacts
- **Statistical Analysis**: Exclusion criteria based on validated quality metrics and artifact identification
- **Software Engineering**: Python-based wrappers and automated processing pipelines for scalable QC implementation
- **Educational Materials**: Training sessions and protocols for reproducible QC procedures

## Outcomes (tech-focused)
- Established comprehensive QC protocol reducing data quality-related biases in neuroimaging studies
- Demonstrated the effectiveness of multiple QC checkpoints in improving overall data quality assurance
- Contributed to the development of automated quality assessment tools for the neuroimaging community
- Enhanced visualization capabilities for identifying subtle artifacts in MRI data processing
- Advanced quality control standardization in neuroimaging through:
  - Systematic evaluation procedures validated on large-scale datasets
  - Open-source implementation enabling widespread adoption
  - Integration of multiple assessment layers for robust quality assurance
  - Dissemination of best practices through educational initiatives and publications
